Chapter 1 Rules

Textbook color coding: Roots, combining forms & combining vowels • Prefixes • Suffixes

  • Every medical term has one or more roots.
  • A root plus a combining vowel forms a combining form, commonly using o.
  • A combining vowel separates a root from a suffix that begins with a consonant.
  • Do not add a combining vowel before a suffix beginning with a vowel.
  • Prefixes attach directly to the beginning and do not require combining vowels.
  • A medical term may contain more than one root, prefix, or suffix.
  • Not every medical term has a prefix.
